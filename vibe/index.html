Super Celik

One app instead of Čelik and TrustEdge ID for Serbian ID cards and residence permits.

  • Reads personal data and photo over a USB reader or NFC
  • Verifies the card signature against the MUP certificate chain
  • Makes browser sign-in to eid.gov.rs work on a Mac

Kachalka

A native catalog of films, series and games with one download queue.

  • WPF on Windows, SwiftUI and AppKit on Mac. No Electron, no embedded browser
  • Curated collections, filters by genre, year, quality and voice-over studio
  • Send a download to another computer at home or control it from a phone

Uniquifier

A web tool that turns one video or image into a batch of unique variations for ad placements.

  • A six-step wizard: sources, 18+ mark, format, file name, extras, review
  • Placement presets, up to five variations per file, style and overlay changes, metadata removed
  • Results as a streamed ZIP or archived to Google Drive
