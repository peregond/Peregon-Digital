Process

One line, five stations

Every project runs the same route. You always know which station it is at and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Brief

    Goal, audience, budget and deadline agreed on one page.

  2. 02

    Concept and script

    Idea, storyboard and approvals before anything is filmed.

  3. 03

    Production

    Shooting, recording or animation with a team picked for the task.

  4. 04

    Post-production

    Editing, sound, colour, graphics and subtitles.

  5. 05

    Release

    Publishing, distribution and a report on the results.

A looped booth video for Monetag at AWA 2024 went from brief to final file in 15 days: 10 for concept, storyboard and approvals, 5 for animation.
